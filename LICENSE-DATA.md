## Exceptions and clarifications

- **Third-party material is not redistributed and is not covered by these licences.** The text of 90 knowledge-base
  chunks derived from third-party documents (manufacturer manuals, bibliographic abstracts and reference lists of
  published work) is withheld. Only embeddings, source file names, word spans, SHA-256 hashes and public source URLs
  are released (`kb/`).
- **Author-compiled chunks.** The released text of the 54 chunks written or compiled by the authors (with the help of
  AI chat assistants, reviewed by the PI) is provided as-is, on the authors' responsibility; the authors are not aware
  of third-party text in them (see SI Section S16 for the verbatim-overlap check), but cannot exclude short technical
  phrases shared with the source documents.
- **Model outputs.** Responses generated by commercial and open-weight models are reproduced as part of the
  experimental record; their use remains subject to the respective providers' terms.
- **Survey and evaluator data** (`stage3_human_evaluation/`) contain role labels only; no personal data are released.
